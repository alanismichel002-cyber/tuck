How it works

Set it up once. Feel it every payday.

1

Connect your bank

Link your checking account in a couple of minutes. Tuck finds your paydays and your recurring bills for you.

2

Tuck splits your paycheck

Every time you get paid, Tuck sets aside money for upcoming bills and your savings goals. You decide how much goes where.

3

Spend your number

Check one number, your Safe-to-Spend, and enjoy it. If it says $40, you can spend $40 without worrying about rent.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing that makes you feel bad.

Payday Plan

Each paycheck is split into Bills, Savings, and Spending the moment it lands. Works with weekly, biweekly, and irregular pay.

Safe-to-Spend

One big number that updates every day. It already accounts for your upcoming bills and savings, so you can spend it freely.

Auto-Tuck

Rounds every purchase up to the next dollar and tucks the change into savings. Add a set amount every payday if you like.

Nests

Name a goal, like Emergency Fund or Spring Break, pick a target and a date, and watch it fill up.

Bill Radar

Finds your recurring charges and reminds you three days before they hit, so you stop paying overdraft fees.

Habit Spotlight

Shows what your daily habits really cost, like two coffees a day adding up to $360 a month. Set a friendly limit and Tuck saves the difference.

Questions, answered

Is my money safe?

Tuck connects to your bank through a secure, read-only link to see your transactions. Money only moves between your own accounts, and only in the amounts you choose. [Add bank-connection partner and security details before launch.]

How much does Tuck cost?

Tuck is free while it is in beta. [Final pricing to be decided before public launch.]

I get paid hourly and my hours change. Will it still work?

Yes. Tuck was built for hourly, tipped, and irregular pay. Your plan adjusts to whatever lands in your account each payday.

What happens if I overspend?

Nothing scary. Tuck shows you how far over you are and suggests an easy way to get back on track. Your bill money stays protected.
